How to play
  • Tap a unit to select it. Double-tap selects all of that kind. ⚔️ Army selects everyone.
  • With units selected, tap the ground to move, or tap an enemy to attack.
  • Drag to move the map, pinch to zoom. Hold then drag (or the ▢ button) draws a selection box.
  • Tap a ＋ plot to build. Tap a building to recruit, level up or buy upgrades.
  • Stand units next to a flag to capture it. Captured villages and graveyards give you more plots.
  • Buildings level up (★) as you use them. Higher levels unlock stronger units.
  • Werewolves are stronger at night 🌙, vampires weaker by day ☀️.
  • Destroy the enemy Keep to win. Lose your Fortress and you lose.
